
OFFICE GAME:
After the office game, I realized that when you start with a gameplay idea, the story can be made along with it, but honestly the story implies a more interesting game than the match 3 premise. When I mentioned to Mavis "you're secretary of a failing company" he instantly thought of some kind of multi-tasking secretary job, which would be interesting.

But then, Plants vs Zombies probably started with tower defense and then added "Zombie Apocalypse". When you talk about tower defense in Zombie Apocalypse, you probably think of a First-person 3D game. Perhaps the silly bit of "using plants" offsets the expectations?

Match 3 games are inherently silly and casual, and should have casual stories to match.

Jake enjoyed the demo, but it just didn't have that magic.
